About SonicKeys

SonicKeys is a browser-based music playground designed to make playing instruments simple, interactive, and accessible. Whether you're using a keyboard, mouse, or touchscreen, SonicKeys lets you jump straight into playing without complicated setup or additional software. Start with the Piano and Drums, experiment with different sounds, and create your own rhythm. More instruments and features are planned for the future.
